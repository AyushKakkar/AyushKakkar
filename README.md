## Now

- **Bus passenger counter.** A private bus operator loses fares he can't see. I taught the overhead door camera on his buses to count: 53 boardings and 45 alightings on the first morning, about nine in ten right on hand-checked segments, zero false counts on 45 minutes of an empty bus. Next: ticket numbers beside the count, then tyre-pressure sensors on the same buses. [Case study →](https://www.ayushkakkar.com/work/bus-counter)
- **Roti production unit.** Replaced a big-name CRM with an ERP built from scratch (120 orders a day, WhatsApp in, Tally out), then put a camera over the line: the model now decides 85% of rotis on its own at 99.7% accuracy, with zero bad rotis passed as good. [Case study →](https://www.ayushkakkar.com/work/roti-factory)
- **Tiffins ERP.** A catering CRM + ERP for a central kitchen feeding multiple sites: recipe explosion, buy lists that net off stock, receiving with quarantine, wastage priced in rupees.
- **A short film** I edited, going out to festivals. Cannes first.
